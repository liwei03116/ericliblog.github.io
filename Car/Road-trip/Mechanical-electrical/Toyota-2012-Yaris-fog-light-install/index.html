🚗 How to Install Fog Lights on a Toyota Yaris (Step-by-Step Guide)

Difficulty: ⭐⭐☆☆☆ Beginner–Intermediate

Estimated Time: 1–2 Hours

Vehicle: Toyota Yaris (Factory Pre-Wired)

Tools Required: Basic Hand Tools

Installing factory-style fog lights on your Toyota Yaris is a great upgrade that improves visibility in fog, rain, and at night while giving the front bumper a cleaner OEM appearance.

If your Yaris is already pre-wired for fog lights, this installation is much easier because most of the wiring is already in place.

🧰 Tools You’ll Need

  • ✅ Screwdriver
  • ✅ Socket wrench or ratchet
  • ✅ Fog light kit
  • ✅ Relay
  • ✅ Fuse
  • ✅ Wiring connectors (if required)

Step 1 - Remove the Fog Light Blank

Begin by removing the fog light blank, which is the plastic cover in the front bumper where the fog light will be installed.

Tip

Use a plastic trim removal tool if possible to avoid scratching the bumper.

Warning

Do not force the plastic clips. They can easily break.

Step 2 - Access the Inside of the Front Bumper

To reach the fog light mounting area:

  1. Remove the screws from the left and right sides of the underbody cover.
  2. Carefully pull the underbody cover toward one side.
  3. Move it just enough to access the inside of the bumper.

Tip

There’s no need to remove the entire splash shield.

Step 3 - Install the Fog Lights

Inside the bumper, you’ll find:

  • Two mounting holes for the fog light.
  • One locating hole for the plastic alignment tab.

Installation:

  1. Insert the plastic locating tab.
  2. Align the fog light.
  3. Tighten the mounting screws.

Repeat the same process on the opposite side.

Figure 1. Body Control Unit.

Step 4 - Connect the Factory Wiring

Each side of the bumper already has a factory connector.

Simply:

  • Locate the pre-wired connector.
  • Plug it into the fog light.
  • Ensure the connector clicks into place.

Note

Check both connectors before reinstalling the under cover.

Figure 2. Fog light fuse location.

Step 5 - Install the Fog Light Switch

Move inside the driver’s seat.

  1. Remove the lower dashboard cover.
  2. Locate connector D160.
  3. Find pins D3 and D4.
  4. Connect wires to D3 and D4.
  5. Remove the dashboard switch blank.
  6. Route the wires to the switch opening.
  7. Install the relay and fuse (if your kit requires them).
  8. Connect the fog light switch.

Important

Verify your fog light kit includes the correct relay and fuse before installation.

Figure 3. Connector D160 and pins D3 / D4.

Step 6 - Test the Fog Lights

Before reinstalling all panels:

  • Turn on the parking lights.
  • Verify the fog light switch illuminates.
  • Press the switch.
  • Confirm both fog lights turn on.

If everything works correctly:

  • Reinstall the underbody cover.
  • Tighten all screws.
  • Reinstall the dashboard trim.

Figure 4. Fog lights operating normally.

🔧 Troubleshooting

If the fog lights don’t work, check the following:

Item Check
Factory Connector ✅ Fully connected
Fuse ✅ Installed
Relay ✅ Installed
Pin D3 ✅ Correct
Pin D4 ✅ Correct
Switch ✅ Connected
Bulbs ✅ Working

Figure 5. Fog light indicator on the dashboard.

✅ Final Thoughts

Installing OEM-style fog lights on a Toyota Yaris is one of the easiest upgrades if your vehicle is already factory pre-wired.

Before putting everything back together, make sure to:

  • ✅ Check every connector.
  • ✅ Verify the relay and fuse.
  • ✅ Test both fog lights.
  • ✅ Confirm the dashboard indicator works correctly.

Enjoy your new fog lights and drive safely!
